About the app

Tartibli Kun ("An Orderly Day") is a daily-routine app for Windows PCs. It shows on-screen reminders at the times you set and reads them aloud, nudges you about eye rest, water and movement during the day, and offers meditation, a weekly report, desktop widgets and more.

  • All your data is stored on your own computer.
  • Optional: back up your data to your own Google Drive using your Google account.
  • No ads, and your data is never sold.
