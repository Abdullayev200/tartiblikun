ENGLISH

Summary

Tartibli Kun is a desktop app. It has no servers of its own and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your tasks, settings and journal are saved only on your computer, in your Windows user folder.

Google account (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app requests only these permissions:

  • openid, email, profile — to show which Google account is connected (name, email and picture). This information stays on your computer.
  • drive.appdata — to save and restore one backup file of your app data in a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

The backup is transferred directly between your computer and Google; it is never sent to the developer. You can sign out at any time in the app, and you can delete the backup in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Tartibli Kun → Delete hidden app data) or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tartibli Kun's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional online features

Some features contact third-party services directly from your computer, only when you use them:

  • Voice (ElevenLabs) and AI assistant (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) — the text to be spoken or processed is sent with the API key that you enter. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Weather, air quality and prayer times (Open-Meteo, AlAdhan, OpenStreetMap Nominatim) — your city or approximate location is sent to get the forecast or prayer times. Approximate location may be detected from your IP address (ipapi.co, ipwho.is).
  • Currency rates (Central Bank of Uzbekistan, open.er-api.com) — no personal data is sent.
  • Telegram — only if you connect your own Telegram bot in the settings.
  • Feedback form — when you press "Send", your rating, message and the contact you optionally enter are delivered to the developer's Telegram. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Your data and deletion

Uninstalling the app and deleting the folder %AppData%\AbdullayevPro\KunTartibi removes all local data. Questions: vector.uvd@gmail.com.

O'ZBEKCHA

Qisqacha

Tartibli Kun — kompyuter dasturi. Uning o'z serveri yo'q, ishlab chiqaruvchi shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va sotmaydi. Vazifalaringiz, sozlamalar va kundaligingiz faqat kompyuteringizda, Windows foydalanuvchi papkangizda saqlanadi.

Google hisobi (ixtiyoriy)

"Google orqali kirish"ni tanlasangiz, dastur faqat quyidagi ruxsatlarni so'raydi:

  • openid, email, profile — qaysi Google hisobi ulanganini ko'rsatish uchun (ism, email, rasm). Bu ma'lumot kompyuteringizda qoladi.
  • drive.appdata — dastur ma'lumotlarining bitta zaxira faylini o'zingizning Google Drive'ingizdagi yashirin, faqat shu dasturga tegishli papkaga saqlash va qayta tiklash uchun. Dastur Drive'ingizdagi boshqa fayllarni ko'ra olmaydi va o'zgartira olmaydi.

Zaxira fayl to'g'ridan-to'g'ri kompyuteringiz va Google o'rtasida uzatiladi, ishlab chiqaruvchiga yuborilmaydi. Istalgan vaqtda dasturda hisobdan chiqishingiz, zaxirani Google Drive'da o'chirishingiz yoki ruxsatni myaccount.google.com/permissions sahifasida bekor qilishingiz mumkin.

Tartibli Kun Google API'laridan olingan ma'lumotlardan foydalanishda Google API Services User Data Policy talablariga, jumladan Limited Use (cheklangan foydalanish) qoidalariga amal qiladi.

Ixtiyoriy onlayn imkoniyatlar

  • Ovoz (ElevenLabs) va AI yordamchi (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) — o'qiladigan yoki ishlanadigan matn siz kiritgan API kaliti bilan shu xizmatlarga yuboriladi.
  • Ob-havo, havo sifati va namoz vaqtlari — prognoz uchun shahringiz yoki taxminiy joylashuvingiz yuboriladi (joylashuv IP manzil orqali taxminan aniqlanishi mumkin).
  • Valyuta kurslari — shaxsiy ma'lumot yuborilmaydi.
  • Telegram — faqat sozlamalarda o'z botingizni ulasangiz.
  • Fikr-mulohaza shakli — "Yuborish" tugmasini bossangizgina bahoingiz, xabaringiz va siz yozgan aloqa ma'lumoti ishlab chiqaruvchining Telegram'iga yuboriladi.
